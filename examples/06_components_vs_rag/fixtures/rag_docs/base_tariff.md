# ACME Freight Accessorial Tariff (effective 2024-01-01)

## 4.2 Detention

Detention (after 2 free hours) costs $50/hour. This is charged for each hour
or part-hour a driver is held past the 2 free hours allotted for
loading/unloading.

## 4.5 Liftgate fee

Liftgate fee costs $75 flat. This is charged when a liftgate is used to
lower freight at a location without a loading dock.

## 4.7 Residential delivery surcharge

Residential delivery surcharge costs $85 flat. This is charged for delivery
to a residential address.

## 4.8 Inside delivery fee

Inside delivery fee costs $125 flat. This is charged when freight must be
carried past the dock or threshold into the building.

## 4.11 Layover fee

Layover fee costs $250/day. This is charged when a driver is held overnight
through no fault of the carrier.

## 5.1 Fuel surcharge

Fuel surcharge, diesel $3.00-$3.49/gal costs 22%. This is applied to the
linehaul charge when the national average diesel price falls in this band.

Fuel surcharge, diesel $3.50-$3.99/gal costs 26%. This is applied to the
linehaul charge when the national average diesel price falls in this band.

## 4.13 Redelivery fee

Redelivery fee costs $95 flat. This is charged when a delivery attempt fails
and a second attempt is required.

## 4.14 Reconsignment fee

Reconsignment fee costs $135 flat. This is charged when a shipment's
destination is changed after pickup.
